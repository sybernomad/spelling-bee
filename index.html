❓ How to Play

  1. Listen: The game will read a word out loud. Click "🔊 Hear Word" or "🐢 Read Slow" to listen again.
  2. Spell: Type using your keyboard or tap the onscreen letter buttons. Click Submit or press Enter.
  3. Attempts: You get up to 3 tries per word. On your 2nd try, a letter grid will appear with hints!
  4. Streaks: Spell words correctly in a row to earn fire streaks 🔥!
