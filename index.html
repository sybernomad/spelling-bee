    ❓ How to Play

    1. Pick a game: 🐝 Spelling Bee (hear it, spell it), 🔀 Scramble (tap the jumbled letters in order) or 🧩 Missing Letters (fill in the gaps).
    2. Learn: The game can show each spelling first. It says the word, spells the letters, and reads a sentence when the list has one.
    3. Listen: "🔊 Hear Word" and "🐢 Read Slow" play the word again. If two words sound the same, the sentence tells you which spelling to use.
    4. Spell: Type or tap the letters, then press Submit or Enter.
    5. Hearts: You get 3 tries ❤️❤️❤️ per word. After a miss, the letters you got right stay as grey hints.
    6. Honey 🍯: First try = 10 honey, second = 6, third = 3, plus bonus honey for 🔥 streaks. Honey grows your bee rank!
    7. Stars ⭐: Spell a word right on the first try to earn a star. Three stars means you've mastered it. Missing it takes a star away, so keep practicing!
    8. Stickers 📒: Unlock stickers for streaks, perfect games, and practicing every school day.
