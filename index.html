❓ How to Play

  1. Learn: The game shows each spelling first. It says the word, spells the letters, and reads a sentence when the list has one.
  2. Listen: Then the quiz begins. "🔊 Hear Word" and "🐢 Read Slow" play the word again. If two words sound the same, the sentence tells you which spelling to use. The word is hidden in the sentence on screen.
  3. Spell: Type using your keyboard or tap the onscreen letter buttons. Click Submit or press Enter.
  4. Attempts: You get up to 3 tries per word. On your 2nd try, a letter grid will appear with hints!
  5. Streaks: Spell words correctly in a row to earn fire streaks 🔥!
